Scraps: a walkthrough

Logging food waste, then a made-up week for a household of two: the footprint, the biggest lever, and the ranking by footprint against weight. 1 min 14 s, narrated, with captions.

Transcript

  1. Scraps is a food-waste log for a household. You note what you throw away, and it shows the carbon and water it took to produce that food, so you can see which habit is worth changing.
  2. Logging takes a few taps: the food, how much, and why. Before you add it, Scraps shows what producing it took.
  3. Here is a made-up week for a household of two. 2.03 kg of food thrown away took 14 kg CO2e and 1,259 litres of water to produce: about 106 kg of food a year.
  4. The biggest lever is beef: only 90 g, but 63% of the week's footprint. Wasting half as much for a year would avoid about 233 kg CO2e. The tips match the food, and the most common reason.
  5. The chart ranks foods by footprint, where beef leads. Switch it to weight, and milk and potatoes come first instead.
  6. The figures are global averages from Poore and Nemecek, published by Our World in Data. Scraps runs in your browser, and the log stays on this device.

Recorded from the live app with the built-in made-up example. The narration is a synthetic voice made with a text-to-speech model (Qwen3-TTS); captions are on by default.
